I am Anupama, currently a PhD scholar at the Indian Institute of Science , Bangalore. My research work focuses on understanding how materials fail and how computational methods can be used to capture and predict fracture behaviour. Apart from fracture, I enjoy implementing and learning numerical methods in general.

Literature has always been my first love. I am deeply drawn to philosophical and existential writers such as Dostoevsky, Kafka, Gogol, and Nietzsche. I write occasionally in Malayalam and English, and some of my writings can be found on my blog.

Painting, for me, is a happy, meditative space. I share some of my artwork on Instagram.
