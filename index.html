I am Anupama, currently a PhD scholar at the Indian Institute of Science , Bangalore. My research work focuses on understanding how materials fail and how computational methods can be used to capture and predict fracture behaviour. Beyond studying fracture and material failure, I enjoy exploring science in general and am always up for a conversation about it over a cup of tea.

Literature has always been my first love. I am deeply drawn to philosophical and existential writers such as Dostoevsky, Kafka, Gogol, and Nietzsche. I write occasionally in Malayalam and English.

Painting, for me, is a happy, meditative space. I am a self-taught artist and I love capturing everyday scenes and moments around me.
